A feed is useful when publishing and reading belong to the same moment. It gives a group a shared sense of now without asking every thought to become a polished article.

Birds starts with the smallest complete loop: a member writes a normal WordPress Post, it appears in the stream, another reader opens the thread, and a native Comment becomes the reply.

From thought to shared context

The value is not the scroll by itself. It is the short distance between an idea and the people who can respond to it. The composer makes publishing visible; the thread keeps the response attached to its source.

Small enough to keep

That loop does not need a new data model. WordPress continues to provide search, categories, archives, users, feeds, and moderation, while Birds shapes the experience around them.
