Birds gives WordPress a more immediate way to be alive. It turns a conventional site into a readable stream for small, continuing conversations, while keeping the familiar strength of WordPress underneath.

The product idea

Early P2 showed that WordPress could feel like a shared conversation. Birds carries that instinct forward with a quieter, more deliberate surface: write something now, see it in context, and make room for a reply.

The result is closer to a well-made room than a busy social network. There is a feed, there are threads, and there is enough structure to help people return.

Three layers, one experience

Birds Theme
The visible product: feed-first home, readable threads, topic navigation, responsive templates, and a Classic Mac OS 9-inspired visual system.
Birds Core
The focused operation layer: front-end publishing and practical post and reply controls, using normal WordPress capabilities and nonces.
WordPress Core
The foundation that remains in charge: Posts, Comments, Users, Categories, Search, Archives, feeds, and administration.

Who Birds is for

  • A small team that needs a shared project room.
  • A studio or publication that wants an informal editorial stream.
  • A reading circle or member group that values replies over metrics.
  • A person who wants a public notebook with a stronger sense of presence.

What you get

A focused front door for publishing, one consistent post presentation across the site, native WordPress threads, a compact topic rail, and a visual identity that feels distinctive without becoming a distraction.

A deliberate boundary

Birds does not pretend to be a complete social network. It does not introduce a custom database, a proprietary content model, or a collection of invisible engagement systems. Following, notifications, presence, and activity ranking can wait until a real site proves they are needed.
