Write. Read. Reply.

Birds is a calm, feed-first publishing surface for WordPress: a place where a small group can share what is happening now, without turning every conversation into a separate application.

It combines the immediate rhythm of early P2 with the clarity of a modern conversation feed, wrapped in a compact Classic Mac OS 9 desktop language.

A small product with a clear boundary

Birds is not a new publishing platform. It is a carefully packaged WordPress experience built from three layers that each do one job well.

  • Birds Theme gives WordPress its feed, thread, window chrome, responsive layout, and editorial surface.
  • Birds Core adds the focused front-end actions: publish, edit, move to trash, and manage replies with WordPress permissions.
  • WordPress Core keeps the durable foundation: Posts, Comments, Users, Categories, Search, Archives, and moderation.

Built for living conversations

Use Birds when people need to publish, read, and respond in the same place: a project room, a studio, a reading circle, a small team, a member community, or a public notebook that should stay yours.

The loop is the feature

Start with the composer. Read the stream. Open a thread when a thought needs room. Reply without losing the context. The interface keeps the important things close and leaves the rest quiet.

Designed to remain WordPress

Your content remains ordinary WordPress content. Birds changes the experience around it, not the ownership or the underlying model. That means the site stays searchable, categorizable, archivable, portable, and understandable to the next person who maintains it.
